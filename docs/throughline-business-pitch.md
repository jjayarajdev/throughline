## The GCC Hiring Reality

GCCs hire at scale across a fragmented vendor ecosystem — and pay the price in
**speed, cost and quality**.

- **Fragmented stack** — ATS, vendor portals, spreadsheets and email each do half the job; nothing tells the full story.
- **Vendor sprawl** — dozens of partners, each with different SLAs, rate cards and contact matrices.
- **Slow time-to-hire** — HRQs sit unallocated, slots go unbooked, candidates drop off.
- **Weak governance** — no reliable trail of who approved what, when.
- **Limited visibility** — leadership has no live view of pipeline, vendor performance or panel utilisation.
- **Onboarding gaps** — BGV, asset allocation and training tracked in disconnected sheets.

## Pillar 1 — PMS — Partner / Vendor Management

Run your vendor network like a portfolio.

- **Onboarding** — structured partner profiles, contact matrix, category / tier mapping.
- **Empanelment** — evaluation period, approval workflow, renewal — full approval history.
- **Engagements & SOWs** — SOWs, change requests, rate cards and PO details — versioned.
- **Escalation matrix** — codified escalation paths so issues never get stuck in one inbox.
- **Auto-deactivation** — inactive partners (no submissions in 30 days) flagged automatically.
- **Performance** — submission rate, conversion rate, joiner-to-offer ratio per partner.

---

## Pillar 2 — HMS — Hiring Management

Move requisitions from approval to offer with structure and SLAs.

- **Requisitions (HRQs)** — capture role, level, domain, location, panel — route through BET approval.
- **Auto-allocation** — RM Owner receives HRQs; if not actioned within SLA, system reassigns.
- **Interview rounds** — configurable rounds per role / domain with criteria and weights.
- **Panels** — maintain panel rosters, history and availability per round.
- **Calibration** — calibration sessions to keep feedback consistent across panels.
- **Slot management** — vendors book against open slots; system expires stale slots automatically.

---

## Pillar 3 — CMS — Candidate Lifecycle

One record from first submission to 90-day post-joining.

- **Cart / Bin pipeline** — approved candidates → cart; flagged → bin for review.
- **Resume parsing** — DOCX and PDF parsed in-browser, no extra upload step.
- **Background verification** — BGV status, vendor and dates per candidate.
- **Personal & document details** — address, family, diversity, IDs in one place.
- **Asset allocation** — laptop, phone, ID card issued and tracked.
- **Training & profile tracker** — onboarding records and 30/60/90-day progress.
- **Joining reschedule** — every change captured with reason and full history.

## End-to-End Workflow — Requisition to Day-90

```
 1. Raise HRQ      →  2. BET Approval   →  3. RM Owner Accept   →  4. Vendor Allocation
   (Hiring Mgr)        (BET Approver)        (RM Owner)              (Partner)

 5. Slot & Interview → 6. Feedback & Offer → 7. BGV & Onboarding  →  8. Day-30/60/90
   (Panel)              (Hiring Mgr)         (Onboarding SPOC)        (TA Lead)
```

**Automated cross-cutting actions**
- **Hourly** — expire stale slots · drop candidates after 3 rejections · refresh slot counts.
- **Daily** — reassign aged HRQs · reject stale on-hold candidates · deactivate inactive partners · trigger escalations.
- **Always** — audit log of every state change · email notifications · role-based dashboards.

## Interview Operations at Scale

The hardest part of GCC hiring is interview logistics. Throughline automates it.

- **Slot pools** — panels publish slots, vendors book; double-booking is impossible.
- **Auto-expiry** — unbooked slots expire after a configurable window — no lost capacity.
- **Round configuration** — different rounds per role / domain with weighted criteria.
- **Structured feedback** — feedback templates per round; aggregated to hire / no-hire.
- **Calibration sessions** — keep different panels scoring the same candidate consistently.
- **Panel history** — full record of who interviewed whom — useful for re-interviews and audits.

## Onboarding & Post-Joining

Day 0 to Day 90 — every joiner ends on the right side of compliance.

- **Background verification** — BGV vendor, status, dates and result tracked per candidate.
- **Personal & document details** — address, family, diversity, ID documents — uploaded once.
- **Asset allocation** — laptop, phone, access cards issued and tracked.
- **Training records** — onboarding sessions attended; gaps flagged automatically.
- **Profile tracker** — 30 / 60 / 90-day post-joining progress with reminders.

## Governance · Audit · Compliance

Audit-ready by design — not a feature you switch on.

- **Full audit trail** — every state change (candidate, engagement, panel, bin, PO, joining) recorded with who, when and why.
- **Granular RBAC** — permission matrix at module × form level. Different roles, same screens, different powers.
- **Approval workflows** — BET approval, RM Owner accept, partner empanelment — each step gated and logged.
- **Configurable masters** — domains, skills, locations, levels, status codes — configurable without code changes.

## Security & Enterprise Readiness

- **Single Sign-On** — SAML 2.0 (Okta), Azure AD and JWT — fits your existing identity stack.
- **Role-based access** — permission matrix in the database; UI mirrors the same flags.
- **Cloud-native** — runs on Azure: SQL Database, Blob Storage, Functions, App Service.

- **Observability** — structured logging with daily rolling files; per-request timing.

- **Open API** — Swagger / OpenAPI auto-published — easy to integrate with HRMS, BI, etc.
- **Data residency** — stays in your Azure tenant — your data, your geography.
